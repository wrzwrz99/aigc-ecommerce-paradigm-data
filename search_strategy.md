# Search Strategy

## Database and interface

- Database: CNKI (China National Knowledge Infrastructure) journal database — 中国知网期刊数据库
- Search field: Topic (主题, SU), which matches the title, keyword and abstract fields
- Search mode: exact match (精确); logical connectors AND/OR as below

## Search expression

**(Technology family) AND (Context family)**, requiring at least one term from each family in the topic field.

### Technology family (OR)

- 生成对抗网络 (generative adversarial network / GAN)
- 生成式模型 / 生成模型 (generative model)
- 生成式预训练 (generative pretraining)
- AIGC / 生成式人工智能 (generative artificial intelligence)
- 大语言模型 (large language model / LLM)
- 虚拟主播 (virtual anchor)
- AI导购 / 智能导购 (AI shopping assistant)
- 智能客服 (intelligent customer service)

### E-commerce context family (OR)

- 电子商务 (e-commerce)
- 网络购物 / 在线购物 (online shopping)
- 消费者行为 (consumer behavior)
- 用户行为 (user behavior)
- 购买意愿 (purchase intention)
- 直播电商 / 直播带货 (livestreaming commerce)
- 虚拟试穿 (virtual try-on)

## Filters

- Publication window: 2015-01-01 to the retrieval date
- Document type: journal articles only; news items, book reviews, conference abstracts and other non-academic records excluded
- Journal scope: CSSCI source journals and CSSCI extension journals in library/information science, management and e-commerce, journalism and new media, and economics and trade
- Early-window continuity: because the term "AIGC" was not widely used before 2018, early academic terms (GAN, generative models, generative pretraining) were added to the technology family to ensure coverage before the 2018 breakpoint
- De-duplication: by title
- Snowballing: key references cited by retrieved articles were traced and included
